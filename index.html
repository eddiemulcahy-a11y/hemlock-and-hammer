About Hemlock & Hammer. H&H is a Whidbey Island construction firm specializing in sympathetic alterations to architecturally significant homes, accessibility installations, and pocket-neighborhood renovation work. We approach every project with the discipline of a pre-construction scoping document — surfacing risks, clarifying scope, and respecting the design integrity of the homes we work in.

Eddie Mulcahy, sole managing member. Tristan Johnson, General Contractor.
