02 · Known Gating Items

From Tristan's initial read. These shape the timeline conversation with the homeowner.

⚠ Long-Cycle Permit Project

Tristan estimates 6–8 months for permits. Langley sits on Saratoga Passage — a stairway from a residence "down to the water" almost certainly triggers shoreline jurisdiction (Shoreline Management Act, WAC 173-26) and likely critical-areas review (geologically hazardous areas, marine bluff buffers, possibly fish & wildlife habitat). Set timeline expectations with the homeowner upfront. Homeowners who haven't been through shoreline permitting before consistently underestimate the cycle.

02.1 Pre-Bid Costs the Homeowner Should Expect

  • Permit fees — City of Langley + likely Island County critical areas, possibly WA DOE (Department of Ecology) JARPA if shoreline armoring is in scope
  • Geotechnical report — Required for any structure on a marine bluff. Typical fee range is meaningful (4–5 figures depending on slope stability questions). Homeowner pays direct.
  • Structural engineering — Stamped drawings for foundation/anchor design and stair structure. Sub fee, can be H&H pass-through or homeowner-direct.
  • Survey — May be required if property pins or shoreline OHWM (ordinary high water mark) aren't established.

02.2 Likely Permit Stack

  • Shoreline Substantial Development Permit OR Shoreline Exemption (depends on cost threshold and scope)
  • Critical Areas review — geo hazard, marine bluff buffer
  • City of Langley building permit
  • Possible JARPA if any in-water work or armoring is proposed

03 · Open Questions for the Homeowner

Capture before site walk. Most of these unblock the bid.

03.1 Project Scope

  • What's currently there? (Existing trail, old stairs to demo, fully greenfield?)
  • How far down is the water? Approximate vertical drop and run.
  • Is the destination a beach, dock, or in-water structure?
  • Materials preference? (Pressure-treated, ipe, steel, helical pier foundation, etc.)
  • Lighting? Handrails? Landings? Any covered or partial-cover sections?

03.2 Property & Permits

  • Property address (full) — confirms parcel for permit research
  • Has any prior permitting work been done on the property in the last 5 years?
  • Is there a recent geotechnical or shoreline study on file?
  • Are there existing restrictions (easements, HOA, conservation)?
  • Any prior contractor estimates we should know about?

03.3 Timeline & Decision

  • What's driving the timeline? (Sale prep, accessibility, recreation, insurance?)
  • Is this homeowner aware of the 6–8 month permit reality?
  • Decision-maker — single owner or co-owners both signing?
  • Budget signal — has the homeowner thought about a number, or are they looking to H&H to scope the cost?
